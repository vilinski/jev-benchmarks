Method and data

Same 1,100 public questions (22 datasets × 50 test items, seed 7), one per request through the jev CLI. Accuracy is fractional top-option accuracy, Brier is the sum of squared errors over options. Failed, refused or unsupported answers score as uniform. Timings include process start, TLS, network and retries; successful calls only. Native decision probabilities, DIY token logprobs and Celeris generative self-reported probabilities are distinct methods. Statistical intervals in the report use 2,000 seeded bootstrap resamples. Partial runs stay out of this plot.

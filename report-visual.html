How cost is calculated

Per call: ((input − cached) × input price + cached × cache price + output × output price) / 1,000,000. The mean of calls with known usage is multiplied by 1,000. Output usage can be nonzero when its price is $0. Missing usage is not free usage. Missing cache rates use the normal input rate in the benchmark estimate, and remain unknown in the published-cache-price view. The Celeris generative models bill their reported diffusion-token usage. Failed or invalid replies can still have incurred usage.

API-reported billing is a separate measurement and may cover fewer calls. These estimates exclude tax, payment fees, credits and Cloudflare’s daily free allowance. They do not promise 1,000 successful decisions. Prices checked 2026-10-15; every route’s source is in the price audit.

The sample

50 items from each of 22 public jev-bench test splits; seed 7. Identical ordered question IDs across complete routes. Each public request contains one question. The private-label set has 60 tasks × 3 repeats.

Scoring

Fractional credit for tied top options; uniform fallback for unsupported, failed and refused answers. Brier is the sum of squared errors across answer options. Missing score-level counts in historical failed rows are excluded from scoring; coverage and denominators are retained in downloads.

Versions and uncertainty

Jev 1.14 uses pinned IDs jev-1.14.0 and typesafe/jev-1.14. Jev 1.13 remains a separate model. Aliases are not extra models. Paired comparisons match exact question IDs and repeat numbers. Statistical intervals use 2,000 seeded bootstrap samples and remain in the downloadable data. Overlapping or small differences need your own evaluation.

Time, access and scope

Measurements share one machine and network. Historical and new runs occurred at different times; latency is not a controlled simultaneous gateway experiment. Partial Cloudflare runs are shown as progress, excluded from complete rankings. Span-01 and Span-01 Lite were excluded at the owner’s request for quality and coverage; original measurements are retained in the local archive.
